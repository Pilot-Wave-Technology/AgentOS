How the Taximeter works

{# EVERY NUMBER BELOW IS RENDERED FROM `tx.scoring`, never typed. The weights, the decision points, the priority points and the clamp bounds come from categories.py and v2/points.py, so a change to a weight cannot leave this panel describing the old one. test_taximeter_explainer_render.py asserts each rendered value equals its constant. #}

Everything in a story belongs to the person it is assigned to

Its completion, its priority points, its cost and time, and every decision answered in it — whoever answered. The kernel, the goal orchestrator, your assistant and a standing grant are all working for you, so what they do on your story counts for you.

The score

Your share of the team’s work: the number of people shown, multiplied by your rate, divided by everyone’s rate. Your rate is your total over your own active weeks, so joining partway through a period does not read as working less. Every column adds up to the number of people, which is why a fair share is exactly 1.0.

Oversight — {{ (tx.scoring.categories.oversight * 100)|round|int }}% of Overall

The decisions made in stories assigned to you: plans approved, work accepted or sent back, merges approved, escalations and questions answered, runs paused or steered. Plus setting up goals, teams and stories. Decisions in goals that finished more work this period count for more.

{% for kind, pts in tx.scoring.decisions|dictsort(by='value', reverse=true) %}{{ kind }} {{ pts }}{% if not loop.last %} · {% endif %}{% endfor %}

Throughput — {{ (tx.scoring.categories.throughput * 100)|round|int }}% of Overall

Stories assigned to you that reached done or reviewed, and errand runs that finished, each weighted by priority ({% for name, pts in tx.scoring.priority|dictsort(by='value') %}{{ name }} {{ pts }}{% if not loop.last %}, {% endif %}{% endfor %}). Engine merge stories are not counted: they integrate work that was already counted.

Efficiency — {{ (tx.scoring.categories.efficiency * 100)|round|int }}% of Overall

Priority-weighted work delivered per hour, from when a story started to when it finished, compared with others who finished work this period. Rates are held within {{ tx.scoring.clamp_low }}× and {{ tx.scoring.clamp_high }}× the median, so one unusually fast story cannot take the column.

Cost is shown beside the scores and is not scored. Work done in VS Code records no model spend at all, so its cost is unknown rather than zero — and ranking people on a measurement that exists for only some of them would score the lane they worked in rather than the work they did.

Goal weight

One, plus the work items finished in that goal this period. It is computed from the work itself each time the page is drawn — no model is asked, and a goal that finished nothing still weighs one, because the decisions made in it were still decisions.

Not counted

Deleted and archived goals, which give no credit and never appear here. Engine merge stories, as above. And machine bookkeeping — a run proposing its own completion is not a decision anybody made.

This reflects activity patterns across the platform, not a performance ranking - different roles naturally show different patterns across these three areas by design.

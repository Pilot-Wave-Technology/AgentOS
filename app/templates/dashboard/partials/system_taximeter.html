How the Taximeter works

Oversight

How actively you're steering the work: creating goals, managing who's on the team, and having interactions within the goal thread.

Throughput

How many stories you've completed: stories are given a weightage based on how impactful a story is.

Efficiency

How fast and how cost-effectively those stories were completed.

This reflects activity patterns across the platform, not a performance ranking - different roles naturally show different patterns across these three areas by design.

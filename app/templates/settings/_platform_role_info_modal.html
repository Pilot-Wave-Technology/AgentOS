What can each role do?

platform.editor has the same Settings page access as platform.member: only My Agents, Help & Explainability, and Model Pricing (read-only). Users, Signup Requests, the Agent/Skill/Tool Registry, Memory Prompts, Claude Phases, Integrations, Kill Switch, Dashboard, and Council Runs are all platform.admin-only. Editor's one distinguishing capability is goal creation (a general goal-lifecycle action, not a Settings page).

This is platform-wide access - it's independent of any specific goal's membership. Inside a goal, day-to-day work (stories, tasks, chat, resources) is governed by that goal's own goal.member / goal.manager roles; a platform.editor or platform.admin gets no bypass there unless they're also an actual member - see the "What can roles do?" button on any goal's Members page. The platform-wide exceptions are goal delete/pause/resume (Kill Switch — platform.admin-only, works regardless of goal membership, same as system freeze) and the Security/Goal Review/Platform Research Councils, which are platform.admin-only even for a goal's own members or managers.
